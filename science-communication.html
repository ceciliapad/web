Outreach

Science Communication

Articles, podcasts and interviews sharing our research with a wider audience.

We believe research should reach people beyond academia. Cecilia was a Public Scholars Fellow at Sapiens where she trained in science communication, and on how to tell better stories. She also worked as assistant producer of the Many Minds Podcast. The lab regularly discusses scientific findings in the media to put them into context and reach people outside the academic bubble, and organises workshops and events for the broader public. You can find popular science articles, podcasts and interviews below.

In English

Articles

How Societies Morph with the Seasons

Cecilia wrote an article for Sapiens magazine on the seasonal flexibility in social organization in many societies – and how fixed political structures are an outlier in human history.

Racing the clock

Cecilia and her friend Erika Freeman wrote a story for Science Magazine, discussing the challenges posed by the academic system on women's reproductive decisions (or options!).

Societies of Perpetual Movement

Cecilia wrote an article for Aeon Magazine on why societies, throughout history, have refused to become sedentary, and the importance of mobility in human evolution.

Celebrity Status Almost Ruined Ancient DNA Research

Cecilia wrote an article for SAPIENS magazine on how the history and celebrity status of ancient DNA research, since its origins has shaped the workings of the scientific industry.

Did Humanity Really Arise in One Place?

Cecilia wrote an article for SAPIENS magazine on how recent archaeological, genetic and paleoenvironmental evidence has forced us to rethink the origins of our species.

Podcasts and Interviews

Hunting, Gathering, and the Fluidity of Gender Roles

Cecilia wrote and recorded a podcast episode for Sapiens Magazine where she talks with Prof Karen Kramer about the origins and evolution of division of labour in hunter-gatherer societies, and its implications for understanding gender roles.

Did we evolve to live in small groups?

Cecilia was interviewed by Ilari Mäkela for the On Humans podcast, discussing her experiences among hunter-gatherers, what a mobile lifestyle might mean to them, and what we are beginning to learn about the ways in which these societies live, and thrive.

The Early Human Social Network

Cecilia was interviewed for American Scientist's special issue on how simulations can address humanity's biggest questions, and discussed how computational modelling (including our own work) has advanced the study of Human Evolution.

The Early Human Social Network

An extended version of the interview published in American Scientist is also available as a podcast.

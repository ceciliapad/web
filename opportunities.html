Opportunities

Come join the GPS Lab!

We are always looking for curious and motivated people who want to explore how humans and other hominins evolved, moved and adapted, using creative and interdisciplinary methods. If our questions excite you, get in touch.

What you could work on

Research themes

Projects in the lab can draw on population genetics, computational modelling, archaeology and fieldwork. Broadly, we are interested in:

What drives human population dynamics and mobility
Interactions within and between human populations and other hominins, and their consequences for our genome, culture and morphology
How humans and other primates adapted to diverse environments and changing climates
How traces of past behaviour can be recovered from the archaeological record

These are broad themes rather than fixed projects. If any of them sparks your curiosity, get in touch and we can work through ideas together to shape a project that fits your interests.

Ways to join

Opportunities at every career stage

MSc theses

Master's students, including from the ASHE programme in Tübingen, are welcome to do their thesis research with us on any of the lab's topics.

PhD projects

We welcome prospective PhD students interested in developing their own project within the lab's research themes. Specific funded PhD positions will be advertised here.

Postdoc fellowships

We are happy to support applications for independent postdoctoral fellowships hosted in the lab, such as:

  • DFG Walter Benjamin Programme
  • Humboldt Research Fellowship
  • MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships

Internships

Short research internships are a great way to gain hands-on experience with genetic data, modelling or archaeological datasets, and to see whether research is for you.

How to get in touch

Interested? Send an email

  1. Write to cecilia.padilla-iglesias@uni-tuebingen.de, saying which kind of position you are interested in.
  2. Tell us briefly about your background and the questions or topics that excite you.
  3. Attach your CV. For fellowships, mention the scheme and its deadline so we can plan the application together.

We look forward to hearing from you

Prospective students, postdocs and interns are always welcome to reach out.
